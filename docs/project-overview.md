# Radical changes: project overview

**Radical changes** is the owner's project for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, challenge **"The Earth Information Jukebox"**, at the **Chicago** local event.

## Event facts (checked 2026-11-14)

- Event: NASA Space Apps Chicago, November 14–15, 2026, in-person and virtual. Page: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/chicago/
- Venue changed on day one to **The Ferris, 1 West Monroe Street** ("hosted by Ferris & Co"). Partner: Northwestern University's CIERA.
- Submission opens Nov 14 9:00 AM and closes **Nov 15 11:59 PM local time**. The Chicago local judging time may be earlier; it was not posted.
- Chicago Discord: the page shows `discord.gg/Wtn87ufBAy` but links to `discord.gg/sBAfwhFjx`; unclear which is current.
- Results: Global Nominees Dec 3, 2026; Global Finalists Dec 17, 2026; 10 Global Winners Jan 26, 2027.

## The challenge in one paragraph

Build an "Earth Jukebox" (web app, desktop app, API, or a curated playlist with an explanation) that pairs NASA Earth Information Center (EIC) visualization frames with sonification, ideally in real time. Access for blind and low-vision users is "a core theme". Use at least one NASA resource from the challenge's Resources tab (11 items, all from NASA Goddard's Scientific Visualization Studio). Judging scores Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation from 1 to 5. The demo is a video of 30 seconds or less, or at most 7 slides.

## Rules that matter for this project

- Any AI ("Super Intelligence", SI) use must be disclosed; SI-generated audio must be labelled in its text and metadata.
- No copyrighted music or samples without permission.
- No work before the hackathon starts.
