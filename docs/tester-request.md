# Tester request

Hello! We are a student team making "Radical changes", a website that turns NASA's night-lights satellite images (2012-2023) into a 27-second song, so people can hear how light pollution is changing.

We would love blind and low-vision listeners to try it and tell us:

- Can you use the site with your screen reader and keyboard?
- Does the song help you hear the change? What is confusing or unpleasant?

Try it: https://radicalchanges.earth

Reply with a comment or message, as short as you like. This is anonymous: we do not ask for your name, and we keep no personal data. You do not need to share anything about yourself.

Thank you!
