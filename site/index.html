    How the music is made

    • Rhythm

      How far the frame’s main color is from red. The farther, the more offbeat the rhythm.

    • Melody and key

      The main color is compared with the average color of each column, left to right. That sets the melody’s pitch and the key within the list of keys.

    • Tempo

      Vibrancy is averaged 50/50 with the size of change from the previous frame.

    • Major or minor

      Happiness is the share of pixels at brightness 127 or more. More light means less happy, so minor.

    • Short or long notes

      Uniqueness compares a bright spot with the light around it. It sets short or long notes.

    • Instrument family

      Contrast compares connected full-brightness pixels with dark pixels. High contrast gives synth and piano; low contrast gives harp, bells, flute and sustain strings.
