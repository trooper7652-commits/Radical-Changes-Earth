# Three visual directions (mockups, not the final site)

Open `a/index.html`, `b/index.html`, `c/index.html`. Screenshots: `desktop.png` (1440 wide, full page) and `mobile.png` (390 wide) in each folder. Text under the quote and in the frame descriptions is placeholder. Frames are crops of data/frames (x 1920-5760) scaled to 1280x720.

## A. Night Ledger
Idea: a quiet editorial page. A huge italic quote sits over a dimmed night-lights frame; the rest reads like a ledger with a sticky heading rail and the six rules as ruled rows.
Palette: ink blue-black #090c14, warm paper white #f3ecdf, amber #ffb347. Type: Newsreader (serif display, italic quote) with Instrument Sans.
Light pollution: a field of amber dots rises from the bottom of the hero and slowly breathes (opacity/scale), like a city's glow creeping up into a dark sky. Amber is the only accent, so light is the only color.
Motion: one slow dot-field pulse; nothing else.

## B. Horizon Glow
Idea: the hero is Earth seen from space, a huge dark planet arc with the night-lights frame inside it and a glowing rim. Centered, heavy wide type; the six rules are cards topped by bars that grow longer with each rule.
Palette: near-black #05060d, sodium orange #ff8a2b, LED cool blue #8fd4ff, text #eef2ff. Type: Syne (wide, heavy) with Hanken Grotesk.
Light pollution: the orange rim glow and horizon bloom is skyglow; orange (old sodium lamps) against cool blue (newer LEDs) puts the change in lighting in the palette itself.
Motion: the horizon glow slowly breathes (transform/opacity).

## C. Star Chart
Idea: the only light page. A dark star-field hero whose sky turns amber toward the horizon so the stars wash out, then the page lands on pale chart paper with videos in black "windows" with offset burnt-orange shadows. Rules are a two-column margin-rule list.
Palette: deep navy #0c1328, chart paper #eef0f4, ink #10172b, burnt orange #c2410c. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (headings) with Source Serif 4 (body and quote).
Light pollution: stars are visible at the top and disappear into skyglow below, the plain experience of a polluted sky; a three-frame strip (2012, 2023, change map) closes the hero.
Motion: stars twinkle slowly (opacity only).

All three: skip link, visible focus, native video controls and selects (keyboard reachable), text contrast above 4.5:1, prefers-reduced-motion disables animation.
