How the music is made

  • Rhythm

    The main color’s distance from red sets the rhythm.

  • Melody and key

    The main color compared with each column’s average color sets the melody pitch and the key.

  • Tempo

    Vibrancy, averaged with the size of the change, sets the tempo.

  • Major or minor

    Happiness is the share of lit pixels: more light means less happy, so minor.

  • Short or long notes

    Uniqueness is a bright spot compared with its surroundings. Standout spots give short notes, even ones give long notes.

  • Instrument family

    Contrast picks the sound: energetic synth and piano, or calm orchestral harp, bells, flute and strings.
